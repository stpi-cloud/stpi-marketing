# Cold outreach — sequence bank 2 (2026-10-01)

Five more sequences beyond the first volume. Same law: one idea per touch,
one CTA (the demo, free forever), stop on any reply, number-free claims unless
the number is dated and true. WhatsApp before email for ZW/ZM; both carry the
same beats.

## Sequence 1 — boutique recruitment agency (5 touches)

**T1 · email.**
Subject: the 06:00 shift you don't staff
Your consultants start reading boards at 08:00. The adverts were posted at
06:00, and three hundred applications landed by lunch. STPi hunts selected public sources
by default on a schedule, scores each against your candidate's real CV, and
drafts the tailoring pass overnight — your consultants keep the yes/no.
One candidate, free forever, self-hosted: getstpi.pages.dev
Worth fifteen minutes, or should I close the file?

**T2 · day 3, value drop.**
No ask today — one trick from the pipeline: duplicate postings across three or
more boards mean the role is real and time-sensitive. We treat duplicates as
the highest-priority queue, and it outperforms "newest first" for our users.
Log it for a week and see.

**T3 · day 6, the objection pre-empt.**
The question everyone asks: "does it auto-apply?" No — every application is a
card in your Discord behind a consultant's tap. Burnout volume isn't
discouraged in our system; it's unavailable in code. The demo shows the gate
in the first hour.

**T4 · day 10, proof.**
The engine's actual product surfaces, so you can check the claims without
talking to me: the approval card, the 72-hour purge, the CV designs.
getstpi.pages.dev/samples — and the zip carries its own checksum.

**T5 · day 14, the break-up.**
Closing the file: agency hunts are either staffed at 06:00 or they're not, and
mine is automated at $306 a month, about R4,999. If timing is the only issue,
one word back ("later") and I return next quarter with better manners.
