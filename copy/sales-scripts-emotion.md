# STPi — sales scripts: the Emotion Engine set (AFGR)

Companion to `emotion-engine.md`. Same laws as the first script library:
the human is the hero, nothing sends without a tap, the **demo** is the free
forever tier (1 candidate, 5 sends/month — not a time-boxed trial), prices are
USD with the rand as "about", one CTA per artifact. Every script ends in a
question or a demo. The words [in brackets] come from the prospect's own
answers on the call — never improvise their numbers.

---

## 1 · Cold opener (coach on the line, 30 seconds)

```
"{{name}}, {{you}}, thirty seconds, then hang up on me if it's rubbish.

Your candidates aren't losing roles to better candidates. They're losing to
faster ones — the ads post at 06:00, hundreds of applications land by lunch,
and yours are still being typed at night. I run a pipeline that hunts 17
boards four times a day, drafts the letter and CV from the candidate's real
profile, and waits for your tap. Nothing sends without you.

I'm not asking for money. Take the free demo — one candidate, free forever —
and watch three cards land. If they're worse than your Tuesday, delete it.
Did I earn the right to send the link?"
```

Why this beats the feature opener: it opens on **anger** (speed beating
quality), names no technology for 15 seconds, and sells a *look*, not a product.

## 2 · WhatsApp opener (ZW/ZM first, then ZA)

```
Hi {{name}} — quick one. Honest question: how many hours a week do you spend
rewriting the same cover letter across PNet / CareerJunction / the ZW boards?

I run STPi: it hunts the boards on a schedule, drafts a tailored letter + CV
per real match, and waits for your tap — nothing sends without you. Free
demo, one candidate, forever, no card: https://getstpi.pages.dev/demo

If the typing is your bottleneck I'll set it up with you on a 10-min call.
If not, sorry for the ping.
```

## 3 · Discovery — the four questions that do the selling

Ask in order. Shut up after each. Write answers verbatim.

1. **Anger:** "Walk me through one candidate's week with you. Which part of
   that would you never bill a client for?"
2. **Fear:** "How many hours a week is hunt-and-rewrite, honestly? At your
   rate, what's that a month? … And if nothing changes for 12 months —
   better, same, or worse?"
3. **Greed:** "If that typing vanished tomorrow, what's the first thing you'd
   do with the hours? How many candidates are on your bench right now without
   a pipeline — and what's one placement (or one bundle) worth?"
4. **Relief:** "What would need to be true for you to feel safe tapping
   approve the first time?"

If Q2 produces no heat, you have the wrong prospect or the wrong wound.
Qualify out politely — an unemotional prospect does not buy on this ticket.

## 4 · The 20-minute video call (AFGR cut)

```
0–2   "Two promises: no deck, and you leave with the demo running whether
      you buy or not."
2–6   ANGER+FEAR. Their Tuesday, their hours, their number said back:
      "So ±{{N}} hours a week of hunt-and-rewrite — at your rate that's
      ±R{{X}} a month of typing." Then: "Twelve months of the same?"
6–9   GREED. "First thing you'd do with those hours?" Let them build the
      scene. Bench maths: {{bench}} candidates waiting × one placement.
9–15  THREE SCENES (emotion-engine.md §6): (1) the card + the letter that
      used to be typed at 23:00; (2) the 06:00 coverage, the ~85% thrown
      out free, caps + purge; (3) approve one, reject one, watch it delete.
      Invite them to audit the letter against the CV for one invented fact.
15–18 THREE FEARS, unprompted: no auto-send ("no auto mode exists"), no
      invention ("check me, line by line"), no spam flag (your mailbox,
      capped per candidate and per day; replies reach candidate + you).
18–20 RELIEF CLOSE. "Setup is ten minutes and I stay on the line. Demo is
      free forever on one candidate. Solo is $306 a month (about R4,999)
      for three when your bench asks — one placement covers months of it.
      Which candidate goes in first?"
```

## 5 · Closing lines (use their words)

**Recap close (default):**
> "You said [anger quote]. You said a year of the same means [fear quote].
> The first thing you'd do with the hours back is [greed quote]. The demo
> does exactly that on one candidate, free forever, nothing sends without
> your tap. Set it up now while we're talking?"

**Identity close:**
> "The coaches who take this are the ones who decided their judgement is
> what clients pay for — not their typing. Which way are you leaning?"

**Calendar close (relief):**
> "Hunts run 06:00 / 10:00 / 14:00 / 18:00. Ten minutes before 14:00 and
> your first card lands with your afternoon coffee. I do the setup; you
> just answer three questions."

Then **silence**. The first person to speak after the ask loses.

## 6 · Objections are emotion deficits — diagnose, don't argue

| They say | What's missing | Move |
|---|---|---|
| "It's expensive." | Greed not yet bigger than price. | Re-anchor, don't discount: "Against what? One bundle at your rates is R600–R1,500; one placement is more. Solo is $306 (about R4,999) — two or three bundles, and the ten hours of typing is gone. Your own number said the typing costs you ±R{{X}} a month." |
| "Let me think about it." | No peak, or fear-of-action winning. | Diagnose aloud: "Usually that's one of two things — it doesn't feel worth it yet, or it feels risky. Which is closer?" Fix that one. Leave with a dated step + the one-pager. |
| "Send me information." | You informed instead of arousing. | "Better: test it. Give me 60 seconds — I'll show you one card and you check the letter against the CV. If it invented anything, I'll send the PDF and leave you alone." |
| "AI will invent / embarrass me." | Fear-of-action. Legitimate. | Agree first. Then proof, not argument: sample CV vs letter, line by line. The tap. The 72-hour purge. "You approve like it's a new staff member's first week — it earns trust, it never assumes it." |
| "I need to ask my partner / principal." | The veto is logical, the decision emotional. | Equip them: cost vs one placement, who sets it up (us, 10 min), how to exit (cancel in Whop, two clicks, demo stays free). Send `one-pager-partner.md`. Book the follow-up before hanging up. |
| "I use ChatGPT already." | They felt magic + maintenance. | "Who sweeps the boards at 06:00? Who renders three countries' CV formats, purges the PDFs, keeps the caps? This is the difference between a prompt and a pipeline." |
| "Too busy to set up." | Overwhelmed operator. Lead with relief. | "That's exactly whose setup we do. Ten minutes, I stay on the call: webhook, app password, one candidate. Not live in ten, I owe you a coffee voucher." |

## 7 · Post-demo emotional rotation (stop on any reply)

```
demo+1h  RELIEF (voice note if WhatsApp): "Your pipeline is on. First hunt
         06:00. All you do tomorrow is tap ✅ or ❌ — nothing can send
         without you."
demo+3d  PRIDE/GREED: "First cards landing? The reject button is the most
         important one this week — it teaches the matcher your taste."
demo+7d  GREED (their real counts): "Week one: {{cards}} cards, {{sends}}
         sends, ±{{hours}} hours you didn't type. How many candidates are
         on your bench without this? Solo puts two more on the same machine
         for $306 a month (about R4,999) — that's [less than one placement /
         two bundles]. One tap: [Solo checkout]."
demo+14d ANGER (no ask): "The boards posted at 06:00 again today. Yours were
         covered before you'd finished coffee. That's the whole product."
cancel/  LOSS (win-back, honest): "The boards kept running — PNet alone moves
win-back ±70 finance ads a week in Gauteng. Your files were never hostage:
         YAMLs, yours, waiting. Same free demo, no awkwardness: [link]."
```

Rule: every touch = one emotion + one next step. "Just checking in" is banned.

## 8 · What never to say (emotion edition)

- Never invent a coach/agency "already using it" in their town. We're early
  and we say so plainly (`sales-scripts.md` §7) — early-adopter pride and a
  year of locked pricing are the honest version of envy.
- Never promise interviews. Promise coverage, speed, hours, and the tap.
- Never shame the coach's current process. Attack the queue, never the human.
- Never close on fear. Fear opens the wallet's *attention*; greed + relief
  open the wallet.
