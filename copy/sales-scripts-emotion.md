# STPI · Emotion Engine scripts — grounded edition

Use with [current framework](emotion-engine.md) and [operations](../../docs/60-current-operations-2026-10-16.md). These are adaptable examples, not statements about an unresearched prospect. Do not claim a guaranteed emotional sequence or outcome.

## Permission-based opener

“Your site offers [verified service, source/date]. How do you currently handle [relevant task]? If repeated preparation is a constraint, I can show a small example. If not, it may not be a fit.”

Do not assert that their candidates lose to faster applicants, that their business is failing, or that they work Sundays without evidence.

## Discovery

“Walk me through one recent case. What repeated, what required judgment, and what would you preserve?”

“What is the likely baseline over the next year, including improvements you already plan? What new risks would a tool introduce?”

“If useful time were released, would the priority be quality, capacity, evenings back or something else?”

“What authority, data and controls would need to be clear before a small evaluation?”

Use their estimates with date/range. Hours × rate × a stated month conversion is time value, not automatically revenue or cash saving. Subtract added review, maintenance and full provider cost.

## Demonstration

Agree the question and safe scope. Scene 1: a clearly synthetic artifact and source lineage. Scene 2: relevant controls and a negative case. Scene 3: conservative assumptions and a bounded next step. Label simulations and live provider interactions. No real recipient sends, billing grants, candidate state changes or credential resets as incidental demo actions.

Product explanation: “Candidate applications require your approval. STPI's researched sales is a separate automatic workflow under safeguards. Normal candidate hunts are twice daily Monday–Saturday. A qualified result depends on execution, sources and eligibility.”

Offer explanation: “The download is free forever with one active candidate and five counted deliveries per UTC month. The hosted seven-day trial is separate. Provider allowances and safety caps still apply.”

## Objections

**Price:** “Is the issue total budget, the value model or something else? Shall we check a conservative case?” Do not imply one gross placement fee is guaranteed incremental benefit.

**AI accuracy:** “Inspect the claim against the profile. Fact-bound preparation does not independently verify extraction or profile truth; review remains necessary.”

**Risk/control:** show the actual scope, pause boundary, age checks and recovery. Do not promise zero spam risk or universal deletion.

**Another stakeholder:** provide a decision brief with alternatives, work, costs, data/authority and exit. Do not frame legitimate review as a veto to bypass.

**Later/no:** clarify wanted contact and honor suppression. A no is valid; do not use identity pressure.

## Close

“Have I understood your objective and the remaining concerns correctly? Would a small evaluation, a brief for someone else, or leaving it here be most useful?”

Allow time to consider. Silence is not a contest and hesitation is not consent. Never promise a first card by coffee, guaranteed activation time, a fabricated slot or a disappearing forever demo.
