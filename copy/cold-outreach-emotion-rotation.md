# STPI · Researched follow-up, not emotional pursuit

Current companion to [Emotion Engine](emotion-engine.md). Eligible researched STPI sales remains automatic; candidate applications require separate human approval. Emotional variety does not create permission to contact someone.

## Before any touch

Require actual attributable, sufficiently fresh and distinct evidence supporting the message. A source about a public service justifies a relevant question, not a diagnosis of the recipient's private distress. Empty evidence correctly holds work. Do not weaken the gate to achieve volume.

Verify appropriate channel, implemented reply/DNC suppression, sender eligibility, pacing/window and caps. Suppression must remain authoritative across queued sequences. Reply polling is not instantaneous universal recognition. A pause cannot recall already-started SMTP.

## Useful touch reasons

- **Friction:** ask about an observable task grounded in a true source, without inventing lost outcomes.
- **Risk:** answer a requested concern or compare actual alternatives fairly; do not manufacture urgency.
- **Value:** provide a dated, clearly labeled model relevant to their objective, not a made-up industry statistic.
- **Relief:** explain a real control, a safe evaluation or a genuine recovery route with its limits.
- **Closure:** close your own follow-up, not an opportunity you do not control. Honor a requested later date or a complete stop.

One commercial next step per message; retain required unsubscribe/reply-stop handling. The downloadable demo stays free forever with one active candidate and five monthly counted deliveries. Do not borrow expiry or seat-loss language from the separate hosted trial.

Do not switch channels to evade a reply, repeat a no under a new emotional theme or imply a relationship that has not developed. Stop/reroute on observed reply under the actual implemented policy and document its recognition limits.

## Review

Each planned touch needs a reason relevant now, source/date, single next step, suppression check and end condition. A calendar offset alone is not sufficient value. Separate attempted, accepted, failed, unknown and positive/negative reply observations. Do not call composition delivery or any reply interest. Review held work as well as sent work; a hold may be a correctly enforced boundary.
