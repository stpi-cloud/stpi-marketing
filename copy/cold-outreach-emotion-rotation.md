# STPi — cold outreach: the emotion rotation

Companion to `cold-outreach.md` and `cold-outreach-sequences-2.md`. Those
banks are organised by persona. This one is organised by **emotion**: five
touches that deliberately rotate ANGER → FEAR → GREED → RELIEF → LOSS, so no
prospect ever gets the same feeling twice — and never "just checking in".

Laws (all banks): one idea per touch, one CTA (the free demo), stop on any
reply, WhatsApp before email for ZW/ZM, numbers only when dated and true,
the human approves every send (ours about STPi, theirs about candidates).

## Before touch 1: earn the anger (15 minutes of research)

Do not send to a cold list. Send to evidence. Look for 2+ of:

- Their site/socials promise "we apply for you" or personal service — speed is
  their public promise, and the 06:00 queue is quietly breaking it.
- They post candidate-success content but nothing about volume or turnaround.
- They're visibly a team of one to five (the typing bottleneck is structural).
- They advertise CV/letter bundles (Studio margin maths will land).
- They're hiring admin/consultants (the bench is already straining).

Write one true, specific opening line from what you found. Generic = deleted.

## The rotation (solo coach / small practice — adapt the persona banks for studios, agencies, centres)

**T1 · ANGER (day 0, WhatsApp or email)**
Subject: the 06:00 queue
> {{name}} — the ads your candidates want were posted at 06:00. Hundreds of
> applications land by lunch; the hand-typed ones land tonight. Your
> candidates aren't losing to better candidates — they're losing to faster
> ones. I run STPi: it hunts 17 boards four times a day, drafts the letter +
> CV from each candidate's real profile, and waits for your tap. Free demo,
> one candidate, forever, no card: getstpi.pages.dev/demo — worth 15
> minutes, or should I close the file?

**T2 · FEAR (day 3, one number, no pitch deck)**
> One number from coaches like you, then I'll stop: ±8 hours a week of
> hunt-and-rewrite is ±R15k a month of typing at coaching rates — and every
> new candidate makes it worse, not better. The demo shows what one
> candidate looks like with that typing removed: getstpi.pages.dev/demo

*(Use the prospect's own number if they've ever given you one. Otherwise this
illustrative frame must stay clearly generic — "from coaches like you" — and
be cut entirely if you can't stand behind the arithmetic.)*

**T3 · GREED (day 6, the bench)**
> Different question: how many candidates are on your bench right now with
> no pipeline under them? Solo runs three on this machine for $306 a month
> (about R4,999) — one placement covers months of it. The demo is free
> forever, so the bench can judge the cards before you judge the price.

**T4 · RELIEF (day 10, delete the risk)**
> The thing everyone asks first: "does it send by itself?" No — there's no
> auto mode. Every application is your tap; drafts delete themselves in 72
> hours; you audit the letter against the CV before anything flies. That's
> why the demo is safe to just… take: getstpi.pages.dev/demo

**T5 · LOSS (day 14, the break-up)**
> Closing the file: the boards will post at 06:00 tomorrow with or without
> us. If timing's the only issue, one word — "later" — and I come back
> next quarter with better manners. The free demo stays yours either way.

## Persona swaps (change only the greed line)

- **Studio:** greed = margin. "You charge R600–R1500 per CV-and-letter
  bundle and eat the typing. Studio puts your brand on the PDF and your
  writers back to editing: throughput up, price unchanged."
- **Agency / outplacement:** fear = the 38 of 50 who get "a PDF and good
  luck"; greed = a hunt under every affected employee in week one, plus an
  audit trail their client CFO can read.
- **Career centre / TVET:** anger = "graduates skilled and invisible";
  greed = one officer running a cohort's queue; relief = Sovereign keeps
  every byte on their own box for the funders' question.
- **NGO:** fear = "beneficiaries placed" (the report) vs "we can't read 200
  ads a day" (the team). Greed = the pipeline writes the donor metrics.

## Reactivation (dormant demo users, emotion cut)

**R1 · ANGER at the caps (day 0):** "Your demo queue froze at the caps —
that freeze is the pitch: the machine wanted to keep hunting. Two minutes
unfreezes it for a real candidate: [upgrade link]."
**R2 · LOSS (day 5):** "Drafts expire; the boards don't. Want me to keep your
seat, or shall the file close properly?" Stop completely after R2.
