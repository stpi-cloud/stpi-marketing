# Same 7 items on Solo, Studio and Sovereign. The API can read these but not write them.

Q: Does anything send without me?
A: No. Every application arrives on your dashboard as a card with the job, the match reason and both PDFs. Nothing goes out until you tap Approve.

Q: Can I pay in rands?
A: Yes. Every plan has a rand checkout: Solo R4,999/mo, Studio R9,999/mo, Sovereign R15,000/mo. Links on https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Q: Can I pay yearly?
A: Yes, 25% off in one bank-wire payment: Solo $2,754, Studio $5,517, Sovereign $8,280. Whop only takes cards up to $2,500, so annual is by wire. Links on https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Q: Can I try it first?
A: Yes. The free demo runs the whole pipeline on 1 candidate (one candidate, five real sends per UTC month, free forever), or start a 7-day hosted trial: https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Q: What happens after checkout?
A: You land on a short setup page that arms your private dashboard and intake link. Your job seekers get a private intake link; they never need an account.

Q: How does Sovereign (self-hosted) arrive?
A: By WhatsApp. Your signed licence and the Docker install steps arrive together after checkout (and again on renewal). Help: WhatsApp +263 78 460 0904.
