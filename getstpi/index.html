What it does while you sleep

  1. Hunts PNet · CareerJunction · Careers24 · Bizcommunity · iHarareJobs · VacancyMail · JobsZimbabwe · GoZambiaJobs · UNjobs · Impactpool — per-candidate keywords, excludes and city prefs.
  2. Drafts one LLM call per match: relevance score, cover letter, tailored CV — never invents facts, uses only the candidate profile you approve.
  3. Renders print-ready PDFs (ZA/ZW/ZM CV formats) and sends the card: "Accountant @ Acme — Match 87%" with ✅ / ❌.
  4. Only after your tap: it emails the ad's apply address from your mailbox, signed as the candidate, with the PDFs attached. Capped per candidate and per day so your mailbox never gets flagged.
  5. When the employer answers: the reply reaches the candidate and you, and STPi pings you: interview invite, question, no, or a bounced address.

Your candidates aren’t losing to better candidates.

They’re losing to faster ones. The ads your candidates want are posted at 06:00, hundreds of applications land by lunch, and a hand-typed application lands that night. That is the whole problem STPi exists to delete:

1 · The 06:00 queue. Boards reward speed, not quality. If your process starts when you open your laptop, you are queueing behind every applicant who didn’t need to type a letter first.
2 · The typing ceiling. Do your own maths: hours of hunt-and-rewrite per week × what your coaching hour is worth × 4.3 weeks. That number is what the current process costs every month — and every new candidate makes it bigger, so your income is capped by your typing speed.
3 · Judgement is the product. Clients pay for your judgement — which roles fit, what to say, when to push. They were never paying for forty open tabs. STPi hands the tabs to a machine and keeps the judgement with you: more candidates covered, same professional in charge.
4 · Your tap, or nothing. Nothing is ever sent without your approval tap — there is no automatic mode. Drafts delete themselves in 72 hours, sends are capped per candidate per day, and the letter only ever uses facts from the profile you approved. The machine proposes; you decide.

The honest questions

Why is the download not gated by an email form? Because you'd delete our number the second the demo expired, and we'd rather you forwarded the ZIP. The engine runs on your machine; configured LLM, mailbox, messaging and state-storage providers process the data their features require.
What happens to my candidates' ID numbers, emails, CVs? They live in a YAML folder on your box or your private GitHub repo, and that's it. The product ships with profile removal and 72-hour active generated-content access expiry. Downloads, delivered messages, Git history and backups are separate copies, not erased by deleting a YAML file.
Is the demo actually the product? Bit-for-bit — one environment flag separates demo from paid (STPI_FLAVOUR=demo). No feature lobotomy, just a one-candidate / five-monthly-send allowance with no expiry and a lot of purple upgrade banners.
