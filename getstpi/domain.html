Candidate Gmail Default & Custom Domain Sending
Default: candidate’s own Gmail + App Password · Optional: lerato.dube@yourpractice.co.za

Default (recommended): Every candidate connects their own Gmail + 16-character App Password so applications send directly from the candidate’s inbox (giving both candidate and operator a real-world Sent folder). Your agency mailbox is used as a BCC copy and fallback only when a candidate has not connected their own Gmail. Optional custom domain: With custom domain routing below, each client gets an address of their own on your domain — created automatically from their name — and every reply still lands in your inbox and the candidate’s inbox.

1 Domain DNS

  1. Buy a domain from any registrar (a .co.za is cheapest), or use one you already own.
  2. For the relay route, put the domain on a free Cloudflare account and change the registrar's nameservers to the two Cloudflare shows. For your own mail server, keep your existing DNS provider and MX if you prefer.
Already have a mail server (for example, your own cPanel/Exim setup)? Keep its MX and skip Cloudflare Email Routing. Your server must accept mail to the candidate addresses (catch-all or aliases), allow SMTP sending with those From addresses, and provide IMAP for STPi's reply alerts. Use its own SMTP/IMAP login in section 2 and choose “My own mail server” in section 3. STPi will save sent copies through IMAP. No separate relay subscription is required. If your existing provider is Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, it must permit the candidate aliases as send-as addresses; otherwise use a relay.

2 Forward the whole domain to your Gmail (free)

  1. In Cloudflare: your domain → Email → Email Routing → enable. Let it add its MX and SPF records.
  2. Destination addresses → add your Gmail → click the link Cloudflare emails you.
  3. Routing rules → Catch-all address → action Send to an email → your Gmail → enable.

Now anything@yourdomain reaches your Gmail, which is why STPi never has to create addresses one by one.

4 Tell STPi

Hosted (Solo, Studio, Sovereign)

In your Discord workspace press 🔐 Connect. For Cloudflare Routing + a relay, section 2 is the Gmail (reply inbox), then section 3 is domain + relay credentials. For a server you already run, choose My own SMTP/IMAP server in section 2, enter its host/login/password, and select My own mail server from section 2 in section 3. STPi tests SMTP, IMAP and DNS, then posts the results in your channel. If your server only supports SMTP port 465, choose 465; IMAP uses SSL port 993.

Self-hosted (Sovereign)

# tenant.yaml: the relay sends, your Gmail reads replies
mailbox:
  provider: brevo                 # or smtp2go, or custom + host/port
  address: applications@momentumcareers.co.za
  username: 9a1b2c001@smtp-brevo.com
  display_name: Momentum Careers
  app_password: enc:...           # stpi encrypt <SMTP key>
inbox:
  provider: gmail
  address: you@gmail.com
  app_password: enc:...           # stpi encrypt <Gmail app password>
sender_domain: momentumcareers.co.za

Already have your own SMTP/IMAP server? No Brevo or SMTP2GO account is needed. In the paid hosted form choose “My own SMTP/IMAP server” and “My own mail server from section 2”. In self-hosted tenant.yaml, use:

mailbox:
  provider: custom
  address: ops@momentumcareers.co.za
  host: mail.momentumcareers.co.za
  port: 465                    # implicit TLS; 587 STARTTLS also works
  username: ops@momentumcareers.co.za
  imap_host: mail.momentumcareers.co.za
  imap_port: 993
  app_password: enc:...        # encrypt the server's mail password
inbox: null                    # same server is the reply inbox
sender_domain: momentumcareers.co.za

Have your mail administrator verify catch-all/alias routing, “send as” permissions, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and that IMAP login works. If you only have a domain but no mail server, choose Cloudflare routing + a free relay instead.

5 Addresses

  • Automatic: first and last name, lower-case, accents dropped. Ruth Nyasha Mhlanga becomes ruth.mhlanga@, and Lérato Dube-Mhlanga becomes lerato.dube-mhlanga@.
  • Two clients with the same name, or a preference? Set send_as: ruth.m@yourdomain in that candidate's profile (self-host), or ask in your help channel (hosted).
  • When a client finishes, nothing to delete: the address simply stops being used. Mail to it still reaches you.
  • Replies are matched to the right person by the address they were sent to, even when an employer answers from a different system with a new subject line.
