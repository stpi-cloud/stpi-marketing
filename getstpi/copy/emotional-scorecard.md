# STPI · Discovery and demonstration review scorecard

Current companion to [Emotion Engine](emotion-engine.md), 16 October 2026. This is a proposed internal learning tool, **not a validated predictor of conversion or an emotional diagnosis**. A well-run conversation can end with a clean no.

## Ten observable process questions

Score each 0/1 only when the consented record supports it; otherwise mark unknown rather than assuming completion.

1. Did we understand a recent real workflow and allow the prospect to correct our account?
2. Did we identify observable friction without shaming, inventing a causal claim or requiring anger?
3. Were quantities labeled as estimates/measurements and their period, rate and assumptions checked?
4. Did we compare real alternatives, including legitimate continuation or deferral?
5. Did we identify the desired outcome without turning released capacity into guaranteed revenue?
6. Did we explain candidate-only approval versus separate automatic researched STPI sales?
7. Did the demonstration use safe data and show lineage, a meaningful limit and a negative case?
8. Did we preserve the one-hunt-a-day, forever-demo/separate-trial, conditional-source and scoped-retention contracts?
9. Did we surface stakeholder authority, implementation work, exit and material unanswered questions?
10. Was the next step actually wanted, with effective follow-up/stop preferences?

Report completeness separately from factual accuracy. Do not treat a high score as evidence that every claim was true. Score bands, if used internally, are review prompts rather than predictions. Never pressure a prospect to supply the emotional response needed for a point.

## Pre-call note

Record relevant public evidence, its source/date, the question it justifies, a disconfirming condition, known product limits and the decision the call can appropriately address. Do not invent private distress or retain an inferred emotional vulnerability profile.

## Weekly review

Choose a small varied sample: good, stalled/unknown and refused/failed. Check the actual claims against current product facts. Identify one change, its owner and the evidence for reviewing it. If onboarding/source access is the bottleneck, repair operations before raising emotional intensity.

Metrics require explicit events, denominators, windows and cohort maturity. A pageview is not scene completion; an attempted send is not delivery; a reply is not automatically positive. Cancellation can indicate expectation mismatch but is not proof of manipulation by itself. Missing data stays unavailable.
