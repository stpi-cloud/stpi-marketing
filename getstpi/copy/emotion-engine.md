## What this framework is

AFGR—anger, fear, greed, relief—is a conversation and review heuristic. It asks about real friction, real risk, a desired future and credible controls. It is not a validated universal emotional sequence, a psychometric instrument or a guarantee of conversion. Begin where the person is; curiosity, pride, fatigue, legitimate budget constraints and mixed concerns may be more relevant than anger.

Emotion means making a true consequence understandable. Never manufacture pain, shame a person, invent adoption or urgency, infer private distress from public posts, or treat every objection as an emotional deficit. A clean no is a valid outcome.

- Two scheduled candidate hunts per day Monday–Saturday: 04:17/12:17 UTC, 06:17/14:17 SAST. A schedule does not guarantee successful execution or a qualified vacancy.

## The four inquiries

**Friction:** “Walk me through a recent case. Which repeated work, if any, would you most like to change?” Name an observable constraint, not a villain invented to create anger.

**Risk:** “What happens if you keep the current approach? What risks would this alternative add?” Include feasible alternatives. Same or better may be honest baseline answers; do not require worse.

**Desired value:** “If useful capacity were released, what would you do with it?” Separate time value, cash savings and possible revenue. Use the buyer's dated estimates, conservative assumptions and full added review/provider cost.

**Credible safety:** “What would need to be clear before a small evaluation would be worthwhile?” Show scope, authority, evidence, negative cases and recovery. Reassurance must match a real control.

## Discovery and demonstration

Prepare from relevant, attributable public evidence. Two arbitrary URLs do not establish prospect truth. Separate observed facts from hypotheses and ask questions that permit disconfirmation. Obtain appropriate permission for notes/recording and do not retain private emotional profiles.

Demonstrate three scenes: a useful synthetic artifact with lineage; relevant limits and a safe negative case; a conservative value model and bounded next step. Clearly label fixtures, simulations and live interactions. Never send to a real recipient or change billing/credentials simply to make the demo convincing.

A clean sample does not establish future perfection. A public health response does not establish legitimate owner login. A connection test is not delivery proof. A queued operation is not completed work.

## Objections and follow-up

Clarify whether the concern is information, product fit, authority, budget or timing. Some concerns require a product change or disqualification. Provide a portable decision brief for absent stakeholders rather than trying to bypass them.

Every follow-up needs a real reason, appropriate channel and effective stop condition. Eligible automatic sales must honor the implemented reply/DNC checks and their limits; polling is not instantaneous universal recognition. Do not switch channels to evade a no or invent a disappearing free offer.

## Review and evidence

Review a small varied sample weekly. Score process completeness and factual accuracy, not a person's emotions. High scores do not predict a close without evidence. Track defined events, denominators, windows and cohort maturity; missing observations stay unavailable. Refunds/cancellations can prompt expectation review but do not alone prove manipulation.
