# Same 7 items on Solo, Studio and Sovereign. The API can read these but not write them.

Q: Does anything send without me?
A: No. Every application arrives in Discord as a card with the job, the match reason and both PDFs. Nothing goes out until you tap Approve.

Q: Can I pay in rands?
A: Yes. Every plan has a rand checkout: Solo R4,999/mo, Studio R9,999/mo, Sovereign R15,000/mo. Links on https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Q: Can I pay yearly?
A: Yes, 25% off in one bank-wire payment: Solo $2,754, Studio $5,517, Sovereign $8,280. Whop only takes cards up to $2,500, so annual is by wire. Links on https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Q: Can I try it first?
A: Yes. The free demo runs the whole pipeline on 1 candidate (25 drafts, 5 real sends per 30 days), or start a 7-day hosted trial: https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Q: What happens after checkout?
A: You land on a short setup page, add the STPi bot to your Discord and type /claim. Your job seekers get a private intake link; they never join your Discord.

Q: How does Sovereign (self-hosted) arrive?
A: In your Discord. After /claim a private channel opens with your signed licence and the Docker install steps; each renewal posts a fresh licence. Help: /help in that channel, or WhatsApp +263 78 460 0904.

Q: No card?
A: Pay by Lightning (SharkSkin@coinos.io, name and tier in the memo) or Mukuru / EFT to +263 78 460 0904 (reference: your name + tier). Questions: simalidudu@gmail.com
