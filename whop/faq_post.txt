Everything people ask before buying STPi, in one place.

Does anything send without me?
No. Every application arrives in Discord as a card with the job, match reason and PDFs. Nothing goes out until you tap Approve.

Which jobs does it search?
17 job boards by default (22 supported) across South Africa, Zimbabwe and Zambia. One employer is never contacted twice within 30 days, and daily caps keep volume sane.

Can I try it before paying?
Yes. The free demo pack runs the whole pipeline on 1 candidate, capped at 25 drafts and 5 sends per rolling 30 days: https://getstpi.pages.dev/

Can I pay in rands?
Yes. Every plan has a rand checkout on Whop: Solo R4,999/mo, Studio R9,999/mo, Sovereign R15,000/mo. The dollar plans are set to the same value; rand figures are approximate, at R16.31 per $1 (27 Sep 2026).

No card?
Pay by Lightning (SharkSkin@coinos.io, name and tier in the memo) or Mukuru / EFT to +263 78 460 0904 (reference: your tenant id). Your licence is issued once payment lands.

What happens after checkout?
You are sent to a short setup page. Your job seekers get a private intake link; they never need to join your Discord.

Can I pay yearly?
Yes, 25% off in one payment by bank wire on Whop (Whop only takes cards up to $2,500): Solo $2,754/yr (about R45,000), Studio $5,517/yr (about R90,000), Sovereign $8,280/yr (about R135,000). Pick the annual option on https://getstpi.pages.dev/

How does Sovereign (self-hosted) arrive?
In Discord. After checkout you run /claim in your own server; a private channel opens with your signed licence and the Docker install steps, and every renewal posts a fresh licence there. Help: /help in that channel, WhatsApp backup +263 78 460 0904.

What does each plan include?
Solo $306/mo (about R4,999): 3 candidates, 100 sends a month, priority slots; approve in Discord (web dashboard + Telegram when self-hosted).
Studio $613/mo (about R9,999): 15 candidates, fair-use sends, white-label PDFs, LLM fallback chain.
Sovereign $920/mo (about R15,000): self-hosted Docker deployment, unlimited candidates and sends, signed offline licence delivered in your Discord, install help in your private channel. If the licence lapses it goes read-only; your data is never locked or deleted.
