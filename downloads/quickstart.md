# Your pipeline in ~10 minutes

> Want the bits themselves? **downloads/stpi-demo-1.0.1.zip** (sha256 beside it) — one wheel,
> the identical build the paid tiers run (caps in code: 25 drafts + 5 sends per rolling 30
> days until you licence). `unzip`, `pip install stpi-1.0.1-py3-none-any.whl`, `stpi doctor`.
> Full walkthrough with every command: **setup.html** (CV designs in §8).

1. **Discord (2 min)** — in your server: create #stpi → Edit Channel →
   Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook. Copy the URL.
2. **Gmail (2 min)** — switch on 2-step verification, then
   myaccount.google.com/apppasswords → create → 16 characters.
   (No Google Cloud project, no OAuth, no card. Outlook/Zoho work the same.)
3. **Candidate (5 min)** — one YAML: name, country, location, keywords,
   exclusions. Starter that works: `accountant, bookkeeper` + `Johannesburg`
   (or `Harare` / `Lusaka`), exclude `intern, unpaid, commission only`.
4. **Run** — the hosted demo runs hunts at 06:00/10:00/14:00/18:00 SAST Mon–Sat.
   Your first card lands with both PDFs and the ✅ / ❌ links.
5. **Tap** — approve sends from your mailbox, signed as the candidate; replies reach them and you (20/day cap, 5 sends/mo
   on demo — at the cap the card itself tells you what upgrading buys).
   Reject deletes the PDFs. Untouched drafts die at 72 h.

That's the whole loop. Upgrade when your second candidate asks for it.
