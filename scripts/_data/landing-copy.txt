STPi — Strategic Talent Placement Infrastructure — find jobs, write the paperwork, send them. Free demo, no email needed
What it does while you sleep
Hunts PNet · CareerJunction · Careers24 · Bizcommunity · iHarareJobs · VacancyMail · JobsZimbabwe · GoZambiaJobs · UNjobs · Impactpool — per-candidate keywords, excludes and city prefs.
Drafts one LLM call per match: relevance score, cover letter, tailored CV — never invents facts, uses only the candidate profile you approve.
Renders print-ready PDFs (ZA/ZW/ZM CV formats) and sends the card: "Accountant @ Acme — Match 87%" with ✅ / ❌.
Only after your tap: it emails the ad's apply address from your mailbox, signed as the candidate, with the PDFs attached. Capped per candidate and per day so your mailbox never gets flagged.
When the employer answers: the reply reaches the candidate and you, and STPi pings you: interview invite, question, no, or a bounced address.

The honest questions
Why is the download not gated by an email form?
Because you'd delete our number the second the demo expired, and we'd rather you forwarded the ZIP.
The demo runs entirely on your machine — we couldn't collect your data if we wanted to.
What happens to my candidates' ID numbers, emails, CVs?
They live in a YAML folder on your box or your private GitHub repo, and that's it. The product ships with
a right-to-erasure button (delete file = gone, logs keep only an opaque id) and 72-hour PDF expiry.
Is the demo actually the product?
Bit-for-bit — one environment flag separates demo from paid (STPI_FLAVOUR=demo). No feature lobotomy,
just a 25-draft / 5-send / 30-day leash and a lot of purple upgrade banners.
What's your licence stance?
Commercial, source-included, and each released version converts to MIT 24 months after release.
The demo you're downloading may be freely re-shared unmodified — that's in the licence itself.
